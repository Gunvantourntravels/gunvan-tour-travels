Booking & Travel Policies

These basic policies help customers understand the booking process.

Privacy Policy

GunVan Tour & Travels respects your privacy. Information provided through our website, phone or WhatsApp, including your name, mobile number, pickup and drop locations, travel date, passenger details and booking requirements, is used only for processing travel enquiries, bookings and related customer support.

For airport bookings, flight number, flight date and arrival airport details may also be collected to coordinate pickup and monitor changes to the scheduled arrival.

We do not sell customers' personal booking information. Information may be shared only with drivers, hotels or other service providers when reasonably necessary to complete the requested booking or travel service.

Booking Terms

All bookings are subject to vehicle availability, route conditions, travel date, pickup time and confirmation of the applicable fare.

A booking is treated as confirmed only after the required advance payment has been received and confirmation has been provided by GunVan Tour & Travels.

Advance payment requirements may vary by service type. The applicable advance amount will be communicated before booking confirmation.

Customers are requested to verify pickup location, drop location, travel date, pickup time, passenger count, luggage requirements and any special travel requirements before making payment.

Final fare, tolls, parking charges, state taxes, driver allowance, hotel charges or other applicable inclusions and exclusions should be confirmed at the time of booking.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on the booking type, travel date, vehicle arrangement, hotel booking, third-party charges and the time at which the cancellation request is received.

Any non-refundable hotel, permit, parking, toll, driver positioning or other third-party charges already paid or committed may be deducted from the refundable amount.

Where a booking is eligible for refund, the applicable refundable amount will be communicated after reviewing the booking and any charges already incurred.

In case of rescheduling, the revised fare, vehicle availability and any difference in third-party charges will apply to the new travel date.

The exact cancellation terms applicable to a booking should be confirmed at the time of booking, especially for Char Dham, hotel and peak-season travel arrangements.

Airport Pickup Policy

For airport pickup bookings where the customer is arriving by flight, 100% online payment is required before the pickup is confirmed.

Customers must provide the correct flight number, flight date and arrival airport so that the scheduled arrival can be monitored and the assigned driver can coordinate the pickup.

If the flight is delayed or arrives earlier than scheduled, we will make reasonable efforts to adjust the pickup time according to the updated flight status and driver availability.

If the airline cancels the flight, the customer may request rescheduling, travel credit or an eligible refund, subject to any costs already incurred for the booking.

If a flight is diverted to another airport, any revised pickup arrangement, additional distance, waiting time or fare difference will be discussed with the customer before the revised journey is confirmed.

Customers should inform GunVan Tour & Travels as soon as possible about any flight change that may affect the pickup.
